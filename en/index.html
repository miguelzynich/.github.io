About

A designer with an unusual background: 13 years of Art Direction before migrating to UX/UI. This means I join any project knowing both how to solve a user flow and how to make it look visually right.

For the past 4 years, I have worked on large-scale digital products — conducting user research, prototyping, and UI delivery. I am curious about front-end and can read and understand code, which facilitates collaboration with development teams and reduces noise during handoff.

I constantly seek to learn and improve — whether in UX, accessibility, or technology. Currently pursuing a postgraduate degree in UX Design at PUCRS.
